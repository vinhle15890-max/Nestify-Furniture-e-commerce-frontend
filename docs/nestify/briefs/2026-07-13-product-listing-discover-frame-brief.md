# Product Listing — Discover Frame Brief

**Status:** Approved for implementation — Direction 3, **The Held Possibility
Field**. This brief defines the canonical frame claim the implementation and
rendered review must preserve.

## 1. Surface metadata

- **Surface / route / component:** Product Listing — representative route
  `/c/ghe`
- **Primary surface type:** Psychological State — Not Yet Seen
- **Associated state or transition, if any:** Discover / Not Yet Seen → one
  product holds attention → Product Detail / Exploratory Commitment
- **User intent:** Notice plausible objects, narrow the field without false
  precision, and choose one object to understand more deeply without being
  pressed toward purchase.
- **Canonical sources consulted:** `01_Brand_Constitution.md` →
  `02_Story_Bible.md` → `03_Design_DNA.md` → `04_Visual_Grammar.md` →
  `05_Component_Bible.md`
- **Decision Register checked:** D-001. The superseded Organic Editorial and
  playable-room directions have no authority over Listing. The Hero Threshold
  decision does not make Discover an architectural scene.

## 2. Narrative and frame hierarchy

- **Narrative role / permitted disclosure:** Listing owns “I have not seen the
  right possibility yet.” It reveals a category-specific field of real products
  and truthful lenses for narrowing it. It may expose the minimum factual
  identity needed to decide what deserves deeper inspection. It may not imply
  fit, popularity, recommendation, material, or room suitability without data,
  and it does not ask for transaction or simulate Experiment.
- **First attention target:** **Possibility field** — the product-image ensemble
  as one browseable visual field, not an arbitrary featured product and not a
  toolbar.
- **Second attention target:** **Discovery lens** — current category, query, and
  active supported filters that explain which possibility field is being shown.
- **Optional third attention target:** **Held product identity** — the name and
  truthful supporting metadata of the object that earns longer attention and
  leads to Product Detail. Price remains subordinate factual metadata.
- **What wins in three seconds, and through which two signals?** The possibility
  field wins through the largest aggregate image mass and the highest useful
  variation in product silhouette/material photography. Controls, count, names,
  and prices use lower contrast and smaller repeated mass.
- **What supports it without becoming a second protagonist?** A compact,
  left-anchored category/lens context explains the field. Filter and sort
  controls become more visible only after the user asks to narrow or reorder.
- **What remains recognizable after copy and logo are removed?** A truthful,
  low-chrome product field is held against one calm discovery lens and a
  purposeful pause; the composition promotes an object only in response to
  declared data or user attention, never through badges, conversion controls,
  or arbitrary card sizing.

## 3. Composition and mass

- **Dominant visual mass:** The product-image field as a whole. Individual
  products may become focal only through an explicit grouping rule, current
  sort/filter result, or user-held focus; default order alone does not confer an
  editorial recommendation.
- **Counterweight:** A compact collection/category context plus the active
  discovery lens. It does not become a promotional banner or a wall of controls.
- **Visual center of gravity:** Below and offset from the category context,
  carried by the first meaningful cluster of product silhouettes. It must not
  remain trapped in the filter toolbar.
- **Named quiet zone and its job:** **Discovery breath** — a low-density interval
  between the lens and the product field that clears control noise and lets the
  eye enter possibility. It is shorter than the first product image mass and
  contains no decorative card, campaign message, or inactive control.
- **Containment / cropping / safe visual margins:** Product crops preserve the
  object’s readable silhouette and do not imply verified scale. Contextual media
  remains visibly contextual rather than being cropped into a false isolated
  product view. Shared scan anchors stay inside the field; no product touches a
  viewport edge accidentally.
- **Deliberate asymmetry:** One restrained discovery counterweight balances a
  larger product field. Any variation inside the field must be caused by a
  declared group, truthful media role, or user focus—not a repeating decorative
  pattern or random index.
- **Why this is not a default 50:50 split:** Category/lens context is a compact
  counterweight, while the possibility field owns substantially more visual
  mass. Search/filter chrome never receives a column equal to products.

## 4. Spatial and image strategy

- **Density level:** Medium-high after a quiet contextual entry. Image and
  identity density is earned by browsing; controls and metadata recede. Recovery
  space occurs between meaningful result groups or before continuation, not
  after every identical card.
- **Illustration role / photography role:** Photography is primary and must
  preserve product silhouette, texture, orientation, and declared media role.
  Context photography is inspiration, not evidence of size or fit. Illustration
  is reserved for a truthful empty/error state or a specific support cue; no room
  motif repeats across product units.
- **Line strategy:** Apply Visual Grammar §5. Product imagery does not receive a
  universal card outline. A supported filter state or held product may own the
  strongest interface edge; field structure and separators are secondary;
  metadata has no decorative border.
- **Depth strategy:** Apply §6. Listing uses a deliberately flat comparison
  plane; factual photography supplies object depth. Focus may alter overlap,
  crop, or edge strength only to reflect user attention. Do not manufacture
  depth with floating cards, generic shadow, or a room illustration.
- **Light role:** Apply §7 and §9. Preserve source-media material and silhouette;
  do not add a page-level light gesture, overlay, gradient, or glow to normalize
  heterogeneous photographs.
- **Negative-space purpose:** The discovery breath separates lens from field;
  smaller pauses reveal result grouping and continuation. Blank area may not
  compensate for oversized controls, incomplete rows, or arbitrary card height.

## 5. Responsive and anti-AI intent

| Width context | First target | What recedes or changes | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wide / 1440 | Possibility field, supported by compact category/lens context | Search, filter, sort, count, names, and prices remain below the aggregate image mass | normal, grayscale, silhouette, no-copy, filtered state |
| Desktop / 1024 | Possibility field | Reduce simultaneous controls and secondary metadata before reducing truthful image legibility | normal, grayscale, silhouette, filtered state |
| Intermediate / 768 | Possibility field followed by active lens access | Consolidate filter/sort chrome; preserve product entry and held-focus order instead of merely dropping a grid column | normal, first viewport, product field |
| Narrow / 390 | Compact category identity followed by the first product possibility | Move inactive filters and sort into progressive disclosure; keep active-lens summary available; reduce image height or column count before allowing controls to consume the first viewport | normal, grayscale, silhouette, product field, filtered state |

- **Forbidden visual pattern for this surface:** An equal-card wall in which
  every rounded image, long serif name, bold price, border, and hover treatment
  claims the same visual weight while an exposed filter/sort stack delays the
  products.
- **AI smells considered and exceptions, if any:** Visual Grammar §12 applies.
  No exception is planned for repeated decorative rounded rectangles, arbitrary
  featured-card sizing, badge/CTA repetition, meaningless whitespace, literal
  room repetition, alternating promo bands, or generic glow. Bounded filter
  controls are permitted only as necessary interaction containers; that
  exception does not extend to product imagery or metadata.
- **Required accessibility / Failure Behavior / boundary treatment:** Every
  supported lens has a persistent label and keyboard/touch operation; result
  updates and counts are announced without stealing focus; held focus is not
  hover-only; product links retain clear focus indication. Empty, error, and
  continuation states preserve the current query/filter context and offer a
  truthful retry or reset. Unsupported attributes are omitted from filters and
  product claims rather than represented as disabled precision. Staff purchase
  gating is unaffected because Listing introduces no purchase action.

## 6. Product Listing decision questions

- **What should the user notice before individual prices?** The collection or
  category being explored, the range of real product silhouettes within it, and
  the distinguishing image/identity of an object worth holding in attention.
- **What makes one product feel worth exploring?** A legible product form,
  truthful media role, identifiable name/configuration, and a focus response
  caused by the user’s attention or a declared grouping rule. A badge, discount,
  large price, or arbitrary oversized tile does not create worth.
- **How should the interface support comparison without turning into a
  specification table?** Keep common truthful anchors stable—media role,
  silhouette, identity, category when useful, and subordinate price—then let the
  user hold or move between a small number of products. Do not expose absent fit
  data or all Product Detail facts to manufacture comparability.
- **What information belongs on the product unit now, and what remains hidden
  until Product Detail?** Listing may show truthful thumbnail/context role,
  product name, relevant selected configuration if one is actually selected,
  availability if reliably exposed, and subordinate price. Detailed variants,
  description, reviews, dimensions, material/finish evidence, fit reasoning,
  Planner handoff, and purchase controls remain on Product Detail unless a
  future verified common comparison field earns promotion.
- **How can product rhythm vary without making scanning unpredictable?** Vary
  rhythm only at declared category/result groups, by truthful media role, or in
  response to held user focus. Preserve common image, identity, and metadata
  anchors. Never vary size randomly or by array index.
- **What is the visual bridge from Discover into Exploratory Commitment?** When
  one object holds attention, its identity becomes clearer and its single
  product link opens Product Detail. No repeated “view,” “buy,” or Planner CTA is
  needed.
- **What makes this listing recognizably Nestify after removing logo, copy, and
  token names?** Clarity is composed before commerce: a truthful possibility
  field dominates, the lens is quiet but usable, prices and actions recede,
  empty space resets attention, and focus moves one object toward deeper
  evidence without pretending it already fits a room.

## 7. Review handoff

- **Expected review evidence:** Normal color at 1440/1024/768/390; wide and
  narrow grayscale/silhouette/no-copy; first product field at each width; one
  real filtered state; keyboard/touch held-focus state if the selected direction
  introduces it; empty, error, and continuation states.
- **Open assumption or escalation needed before implementation:** The current
  representative category exposes 11 products with names, prices, category,
  availability-bearing variants, and thumbnail/media, but no structured
  dimensions, material/finish, fit, popularity, editorial ranking, or reliable
  facet counts. Category descriptions are also absent. Directions may not rely
  on those fields. Direction 3 has been approved; implementation must not turn
  API order into editorial privilege.

## 8. Approved interaction contract — The Held Possibility Field

- **At rest:** `heldProductId` is empty. Every product uses the same media,
  identity, and price hierarchy; API position does not change mass, edge,
  metadata, or affordance.
- **Pointer with hover:** entering a product unit creates its held state;
  leaving the complete unit releases it unless keyboard focus remains inside.
  Hover changes attention only—never purchase, Planner, or hidden product facts.
- **Keyboard:** focus entering either the media attention control or the single
  Product Detail link creates the same held state. Focus leaving the complete
  unit releases it. Enter on the Product Detail link navigates normally.
- **Touch:** the media attention control uses first tap to hold or release the
  product. It does not navigate. The now-clear product-name link is the explicit
  second action into Product Detail. Tapping another media control transfers the
  held state, so no default or persistent recommendation is created.
- **Neighbor behavior:** while one product is held, neighbors remain visible,
  legible, interactive, and unblurred. They may recede in contrast, but never
  become unavailable or sit behind an overlay.
- **Reduced motion:** edge strength, local spacing, identity hierarchy, and
  neighbor contrast communicate the held state without scale animation or
  choreographed movement.
- **Media claim:** Listing media identifies and contextualizes the product only.
  It makes no dimensional, material, variant-fidelity, room-fit, or popularity
  claim beyond the payload.

## 9. First rendered-review correction

- **Observed failure:** the initial implementation removed card chrome but still
  repeated the complete image → name → price ecommerce anatomy at rest, so the
  held transition sat on top of a generic card wall.
- **Canonical correction:** price is withheld until explicit held attention;
  product identity remains available in a restrained stable anchor; horizontal
  media seams tighten into one possibility field.
- **Neighbor correction:** non-held units retain 80% opacity so local edge,
  type, metadata reveal, and interval—not global suppression—carry the held
  state.
- **Unchanged rule:** all units remain equal at rest. No product gains scale,
  metadata, badge, or recommendation treatment from API order.

# Home Hero — Threshold Frame Brief

**Status:** Pre-implementation; three composition directions follow this brief.
The selected direction may refine placement, but may not weaken any requirement
below.

## 1. Surface metadata

- **Surface / route / component:** Home `/` — Hero
- **Primary surface type:** Threshold
- **Associated state or transition, if any:** Pre-arc invitation; not a
  psychological state and not a Planner preview.
- **User intent:** Recognise an unanswered possibility in their own space and
  choose to explore without being asked to commit or learn a tool.
- **Canonical sources consulted:** `01_Brand_Constitution.md` →
  `02_Story_Bible.md` → `03_Design_DNA.md` → `04_Visual_Grammar.md` →
  `05_Component_Bible.md`
- **Decision Register checked:** D-001. Home Hero is static Threshold, never a
  Planner lesson, simulation, or product demonstration.

## 2. Narrative and frame hierarchy

- **Narrative role / permitted disclosure:** Hold the question before Chapter 1.
  Permit one static, anonymous habitation cue only. Do not show a product,
  catalog, finished room, `imagined`, Planner control, measurement, interaction,
  or resolution.
- **First attention target:** `Điều gì có thể bắt đầu ở đây?` — the open
  proposition, not the room drawing.
- **Second attention target:** A quiet, incomplete editorial spatial study whose
  internal order is light → room → habitation cue.
- **Optional third attention target:** `Khám phá không gian` — a route into
  discovery/collections, never the Planner.
- **What wins in three seconds, and through which two signals?** The proposition
  wins through highest ink/value contrast and an intentionally larger, more
  stable text/emotional field. It must not depend on extreme display size alone.
- **What supports it without becoming a second protagonist?** A low-density,
  lower-contrast, cropped study. It may occupy meaningful area, but its edge
  strength and density remain subordinate to the proposition.
- **What remains recognizable after copy and logo are removed?** An asymmetric,
  incomplete spatial study with one off-axis light source, a cropped foreground
  edge, a held empty plane, and a tiny static sign of habitation — never a
  centered room diagram.

## 3. Composition and mass

- **Dominant visual mass:** The proposition/emotional field. It must outweigh
  the spatial counterweight through at least two signals and remain visibly
  unequal at every width.
- **Counterweight:** One deliberately contained or cropped spatial study. Its
  role is atmosphere/evidence, not illustration-as-hero.
- **Visual center of gravity:** Off-axis, weighted toward the proposition and
  held by the quieter study; never balanced on a central vertical axis or as
  equal columns.
- **Named quiet zone and its job:** **Question zone** — a bounded low-density
  plane between/within proposition and study that holds the answer open. It is
  framed by an alignment, receding edge, value shift, or light boundary; it is
  not leftover whitespace.
- **Containment / cropping / safe visual margins:** Crop at least one
  architectural boundary or use partial containment with clear breathing room.
  Keep text and CTA inside safe interaction margins; do not display a complete
  room rectangle simply because an illustration has a viewBox.
- **Deliberate asymmetry:** Every candidate must use at least two of off-axis
  opening, crop, light direction, dominant plane, furniture placement, or type
  relationship. No centered vanishing point plus symmetric frame.
- **Why this is not a default 50:50 split:** Typography and scene share a
  composition through an edge, gap, light direction, or occlusion. They may not
  occupy equal columns, equal vertical alignment, equal contrast, or equal
  perceived mass.

## 4. Spatial and image strategy

- **Density level:** Low. No product/lifestyle photography. One restrained,
  static, non-SKU habitation cue maximum; it exists from the initial frame,
  carries no fill/materialization, and remains tertiary.
- **Illustration role / photography role:** Illustration alone evokes possible
  space; it is not factual product proof and cannot duplicate Planner work.
- **Line strategy:** Apply Visual Grammar §5. Establish one base structural
  stroke; let receding architecture soften, reserve one foreground contour for
  anchoring, and keep furniture detail below its silhouette. Omit edges when
  values already separate planes. No dashed guides, grids, dimensions, labels,
  or universal-outline treatment.
- **Depth strategy:** Apply §6. Declare a quiet far plane, a lit middle spatial
  relationship, and a cropped/occluding near edge. Use at least three readable
  cues: value separation, edge recession, overlap/occlusion, directional light,
  and density reduction. Perspective alone is insufficient.
- **Light role:** Apply §7. One neutral daylight source must be inferable from
  an off-axis opening or cropped boundary. Its single gesture must travel across
  at least two planes and clarify the relationship between room and habitation
  cue. No glow, warm confirmation, gradient atmosphere, or isolated floor
  polygon.
- **Negative-space purpose:** The largest unfilled plane holds the unanswered
  question and gives scale to the habitation cue; it may not become decorative
  blankness.

## 5. Responsive and anti-AI intent

| Width context | First target | What recedes or changes | Evidence to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wide | Open proposition | Study counterbalances at lower contrast/density; cue remains tertiary | normal, grayscale, silhouette |
| Principal desktop | Open proposition | Crop/quiet zone preserve unequal mass; CTA remains third | normal, grayscale, silhouette |
| Intermediate | Open proposition | Study moves after or below the proposition without becoming first; crop tightens | normal |
| Narrow | Open proposition | Text stays first; scene becomes a smaller, quieter, off-axis follow-on; cue may simplify/omit | normal, grayscale, silhouette |

- **Forbidden visual pattern for this surface:** A centered CAD-like box room,
  especially beside equal text in a 50:50 marketing split.
- **AI smells considered and exceptions, if any:** No exception planned. Reject
  perfect symmetry, default centering, 50:50 split, uniform outlines,
  meaningless whitespace, oversized-serif-plus-minimal-SVG formula, generic
  glow/gradient, and any fake technical diagram (Visual Grammar §12).
- **Required accessibility / Failure Behavior / boundary treatment:** Use a
  semantic exploratory CTA with visible keyboard focus. Threshold has no
  Planner interaction, direct manipulation, or associated failure flow.

## 6. Existing-Hero failure resolution commitments

| Existing review failure | Mandatory prevention in the new composition |
|---|---|
| Centered vanishing point / symmetric box room | Use an oblique or disrupted one-point view, an off-axis opening, and a crop; never show a complete bilateral room frame. |
| Generic 50:50 text/visual split | Give the proposition materially greater visual weight; bind the spatial study to type through a shared edge, gap, light direction, or occlusion instead of a spare column. |
| Oversized serif + minimal SVG formula | Make hierarchy come from contrast, position, quiet-zone framing, and an authored spatial silhouette; display type is one signal, not the whole composition. |
| Fake technical-diagram cues | Omit all guides, dimensions, grids, axis marks, snap marks, and evenly described room edges. |
| Equal protagonist conflict | Limit the scene to a lower-contrast supporting field; its internal order remains light → room → cue while the frame order stays proposition → study → CTA. |
| Weak line hierarchy | Assign explicit structural, receding, foreground, implied, and furniture-detail roles per Visual Grammar §5. |
| Flat perceived depth | Use named far/middle/near planes plus at least three perceptible cues, including occlusion/overlap and value/edge recession. |
| Ineffective light gesture | Use one source-derived neutral light gesture that changes at least two planes and directs attention toward, not away from, the proposition/study relationship. |
| Meaningless blank areas | Define the bounded question zone and the largest empty plane's role before drawing objects. |
| Mobile/intermediate hierarchy reversal | Keep the proposition before the illustration in DOM and visual order; re-crop/simplify the study rather than stack it above text. |
